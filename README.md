## Main economic framework

Incremental profit is defined as:

$$
\Delta \Pi = Rgm - C_H
$$

where:

- $R$ = annual restaurant sales
- $g$ = proportional sales uplift generated by halal availability
- $m$ = incremental contribution margin
- $C_H$ = incremental annual halal-related cost

The break-even sales uplift is:

$$
g^{BE} = \frac{C_H}{Rm}
$$
